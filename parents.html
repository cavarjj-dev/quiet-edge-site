Where to begin

Three steps, starting tonight.

  1. 01

    Find the starting point

    Answer one or two questions. You get the right stage, two matched episodes and one thing to try this week. Open the guide

  2. 02

    Watch in parallel

    Your athlete watches the Athlete Edition. You watch the Parent Companion on the same topic. Episodes by stage
