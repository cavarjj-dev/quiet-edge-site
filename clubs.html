Questions

For directors and coaches.

We already work with a sport psychologist.

Good. A specialist goes deep with a few athletes. The Quiet Edge gives every player and parent a shared baseline, so that one-to-one time builds on common language. Your consultant can assign episodes between sessions.

Our coaches have no time.

Ten minutes a week: one short episode, one huddle question, one note to forward. The coach guide sets out a ten-week plan for each stage.

Will parents take part?

Most start with the car-ride-home questions, because they solve a problem parents feel every weekend. The one-minute parent guide points each family to the right episode.

Is this therapy?

No. It is an educational curriculum, in the same category as a character or life-skills program. The materials direct families to licensed professionals, and to the 988 line, whenever there is a concern.

Does it suit our sport?

Yes. The episodes are sport-agnostic and built on moments every athlete meets: mistakes, pressure, playing time and the ride home.

What results can we expect?

This is our founding season, which is why founding rates exist. We will run a short pre- and post-season survey with your families, so you have your own data at the end.
